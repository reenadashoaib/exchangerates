    Questions about exchange rates

    Why is my bank's rate different from this one?

    RateBoard shows the mid-market rate, the midpoint between what traders pay to buy and to sell a currency. Banks, card networks and exchange counters add a margin on top, so the rate you get is usually a little worse. Compare their rate with this one to see how much that margin costs you.

    How often do the rates change?

    Currency markets trade around the clock on weekdays, so rates move constantly. The converter uses rates refreshed once a day, which is right for planning a trip, an invoice or a transfer. For trading, use a live feed from your broker.

    Where does the history chart come from?

    The chart uses daily reference rates published by the European Central Bank on working days. It covers 31 major currencies. Weekends and holidays have no published rate, so the line skips them.

    Can I share a conversion?

    Yes. The page address updates as you change the amount or currencies, so copy it from your browser and anyone who opens it sees the same conversion with that day's rate.
