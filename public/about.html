About RateBoard

RateBoard is a free currency converter. It shows what your money is worth in 160 currencies, using the same mid-market rates that banks trade at between themselves.

Where the rates come from

Today's rates come from Exchange Rate API, which combines several central bank and commercial sources and refreshes once a day.

The history charts use the daily reference rates published by the European Central Bank, served by the open-source Frankfurter service. They cover 31 major currencies.

What the rate means for you

When you exchange money, your bank or provider adds a margin to the mid-market rate. Use RateBoard to see the fair rate, then compare it with what you're offered. The gap is what the exchange really costs.

How RateBoard is paid for

RateBoard is free to use and paid for by advertising. Ads are always labelled "Advertisement". If you'd like to advertise, see ad placements and prices.
