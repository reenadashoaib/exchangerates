Privacy and cookies

Last updated 27 October 2026.

RateBoard doesn't ask you to sign up and doesn't collect your name, email or payment details. This page explains the data that is used when you visit.

Data stored on your device

Your browser keeps a copy of today's exchange rates for up to an hour, so the page loads faster next time. It also remembers when you last saw the full-screen ad, so it isn't shown more than once a day. Neither is sent to us.

Advertising

RateBoard shows ads from Google AdSense and from advertisers who book space with us directly.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the privacy link at the bottom of the consent message.

Exchange rate services

To load rates, your browser connects to open.er-api.com and api.frankfurter.dev. These services receive your IP address as part of a normal web request, under their own privacy policies.

Hosting

RateBoard is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes visitor IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
